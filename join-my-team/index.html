Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

I’ve never prepared taxes. Can I still start?

Yes, if you are willing to learn the process, complete training, meet current program requirements, and begin with appropriate clients. PCT does not promise instant mastery or automatic approval.

I have a PTIN. What happens next?

You can join PCT, complete training and certification, finish required onboarding and PTIN verification, then activate the same account for Agent access and your client workflow.

Will PCT provide me clients?

No. You bring and build your own client base using your personalized client link. PCT does not guarantee clients, leads, or any particular volume of work.

Do I need my own website?

No. Your personalized PCT client link gives you a professional place to direct referrals without building a separate intake system first. You can share it by text, social bio, referral, or business card.

What happens after a client submits?

The client’s intake, documents, and applicable information are tied to the correct return and assigned Agent workflow. You then work through preparation, review, signatures, payment, filing, and status using the PCT system.

Can I do this as a side business?

Yes. Many people start small and build gradually. PCT does not guarantee income, client volume, or tax outcomes; you decide the pace of your business within the current program requirements.

What does the current 80/20 structure mean?

Under the current program structure, you keep 80% of eligible funded preparation-fee earnings and PCT retains 20%. Referral Circle progression follows the applicable current rules. Full terms are set out in your preparer agreement.

How much is the enrollment fee?

The one-time enrollment fee is $149, shown again at signup and checkout — it's never a different number on this page than at checkout.

Does PCT guarantee income or approval?

No. PCT does not guarantee clients, income, refunds, or approval into the program.

Whose PTIN do I file under?

Your own. You must hold a valid, current-year PTIN, verified through your account, before preparing or filing any client return.

Can I apply before I have a PTIN?

Yes. You can start your application now and add your verified PTIN once you have it, inside your account.

Ready to Stop Carrying the Whole Business in Your Head?

Create your secure account to start your application. Your account, enrollment, training, and next steps stay in this existing PCT flow. Bring your clients when you’re ready; let the system hold more of the moving pieces.
